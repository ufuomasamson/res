DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every design decision must satisfy these principles:

Purpose before decoration: Every animation should reinforce the company's story or explain a process.
Consistency: Colors, spacing, typography, and motion should feel like one cohesive design system.
Trust: Use premium photography, generous whitespace, and restrained effects to communicate professionalism.
Storytelling: Guide visitors through the journey of energy—from storage to transport to delivery.
Performance: Deliver a rich visual experience without compromising speed or usability.
Accessibility: Ensure readability, keyboard navigation, and sufficient color contrast across all pages.
FINAL GOAL

Do not design this website like a typical logistics or shipping company website.

Design it like the digital presence of a Fortune 500 global energy corporation—a site worthy of winning design awards while remaining practical, trustworthy, and conversion-focused.

The finished experience should leave visitors with one lasting impression:

"Res Logistics PTE Ltd is a world-class leader in global energy logistics, combining engineering excellence, operational scale, innovation, and sustainability."
